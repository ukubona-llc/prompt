Civilization stack (Ukubona full model)
Layer I = PersonLayer II = Kin/NetworkLayer III = SystemsLayer IV = RegulatorsLayer V = CivilizationLayer VI = Portal
The six civilization layers map onto the pentad: T covers Layers I–V (the full observable reality). M selects which layer's signals to amplify. V tracks drift across layers. E preserves the identity of each layer's core function. S collapses all layers to a single decision number.
